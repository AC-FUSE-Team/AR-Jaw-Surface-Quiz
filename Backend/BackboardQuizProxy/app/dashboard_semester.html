Prototype scope and data disclosure

SYNTHETIC WHOLE-SKELETON DEMONSTRATION — These fictional histories illustrate how the platform could track learning across skeletal regions. They are not real student records or measured study results.

The jaw activity is the working mobile example. The semester dashboard uses synthetic data to show how the same learning workflow can scale to the full skeletal prototype.

Students 2–5 receive whole-skeleton analytics, Backboard checkpoint analyses, teacher recommendations, and learning trends only. They do not receive PDFs in this prototype.
